Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Vox Reader. We take your privacy seriously. This document describes how your information is handled within our application.

1. Data Collection (Voluntary Feedback)

If you choose to submit feedback within the app, we collect your provided Email Address and Message Text. This data is stored securely via Google Sheets and is used solely for developer communication to resolve technical bugs or answer questions. This collection is completely optional.

2. Background Analytics & Diagnostics

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to anonymously evaluate app usage patterns, monitor crash logs, and evaluate diagnostic latency. This information tracks app performance trends dynamically and is not connected to individual identities.

3. Monetization & Subscriptions

Vox Reader uses Google Play Billing to process subscriptions and short-term feature passes. Financial transactions and credit card details are handled natively and securely by Google Play. Our servers do not see or capture your payment instrument cards, though we process your anonymous digital purchase history to unlock premium features.

4. Advertising Networks

Our app integrates Google AdMob to deliver rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob utilizes temporary mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google's AAID) to evaluate user context and serve appropriate ad placements.

5. Data Deletion Requests

Because your usage metrics and ad interactions are compiled anonymously without an active user account registration profile, we do not maintain a server pipeline to remove isolated sessions. However, if you wish to remove your voluntary feedback entries or email history from our logs, contact us at: appm4ker@gmail.com.
