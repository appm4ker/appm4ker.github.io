Vox Browser Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vox Browser ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Vox Browser app on Android or iOS. You can contact us at appm4ker@gmail.com.

Information you provide

You do not need to create a Vox Browser account to use the app.

If you send feedback through the app, we receive your message, any email address you provide, the app version, platform, and time of submission. We use this information to respond to you and address feedback or support requests. Feedback is sent through Google Apps Script and stored in Google Sheets. Please avoid including sensitive information in feedback.

Browsing and reading

Vox Browser stores information such as browsing history, bookmarks, reading settings, and chapter navigation rules on your device. The app uses webpage content to display it and read it aloud. Text-to-speech is provided by the speech engine available on your device; the engine's provider may process text according to its own privacy practices and your device settings.

When you visit a website, search the web, or follow a chapter link, your device connects to the relevant website or search provider. These providers may receive information such as your IP address, requested URLs, search terms, device and browser information, and cookies, and may collect information you submit to them. Some reading features may request pages in the background, including using applicable website cookies. Websites and search providers have their own privacy policies and are not controlled by us.

Incognito tabs are not added to the app's browsing history. Incognito does not make you anonymous or prevent websites, your internet provider, or other services from receiving information. Cookies and other data may still be available to websites, and bookmarks you save may remain on your device.

Purchases

Optional passes and subscriptions are processed by the store through which you obtained the app: Apple App Store, Google Play, or Amazon Appstore, as applicable. The store handles payment details under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your full payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases, verify transactions, restore purchases, and provide access to paid features. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information, transaction identifiers, and an app user identifier. Purchase information may also be used for analytics and to help prevent fraud. The store and RevenueCat may retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Advertising and analytics

The app uses Google AdMob to offer rewarded ads. Google and its partners may collect or receive device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, information about ads shown or viewed, app interactions, and performance or diagnostic data. They may use this information to provide and measure ads, analytics, and related services, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

When Firebase Analytics is configured and enabled, we use it to understand app usage and improve the app. It may collect app and device information and usage events, such as reading-session duration, chapter advances, and interactions with rewards or purchases. Availability of analytics may differ by platform and app version.

For more information about these services, see:

  • Google
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple

Your choices and requests

You can delete individual history entries, clear browsing history, and remove bookmarks within the app. You can also use your device's settings to manage app data and advertising preferences. Removing the app may remove locally stored data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Incognito mode does not delete data held by websites or other providers.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us, including feedback, contact appm4ker@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. We may need information to verify or locate your request, and some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons. For data held by an app store, website, or other provider, you may also need to contact that provider directly. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions through the relevant app store.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including support, purchase administration, and legal obligations. Local data remains on your device until removed through the app or device settings, subject to backups. Our service providers retain information according to their own practices.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children and unrestricted web access

Vox Browser can access websites that may contain content inappropriate for children. Parents and guardians should consider the app's store age rating and supervise use as appropriate. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through feedback, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or our practices change. We will update the date above when we do.

For privacy questions or requests, email appm4ker@gmail.com.
