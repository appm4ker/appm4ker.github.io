Vox Browser Support

Need help with Vox Browser? Email appm4ker@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, operating system version, app version, and the steps to reproduce it. A screenshot may also help. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Reading and voices

To change the voice, open Settings in the app and select Voice. Available voices depend on the speech engines and voices installed on your device. If a page does not read as expected, try another page and include the website address when you contact us, if it does not contain private information.

Purchases and subscriptions

To restore a purchase, open Settings, select Get access, and choose Restore purchases. Use the same store account that made the purchase. You can also open Manage subscription in Settings for purchase help.

To cancel a subscription, use the account for the store where you purchased it. On an Apple device, open Settings, select your Apple Account, then select Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refund requests are handled by the store where you purchased the app's pass or subscription. Contact that store for a refund, or email us for help with the app.
